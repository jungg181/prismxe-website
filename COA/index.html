MOBILIZATION

Survey the Assets

Record the building condition and known construction interfaces. Asset ID and location, condition and access, photos and open concerns.

Register Defects

Distinguish inherited defects from new maintenance issues. Defect type and origin, contractor and target date, warranty and pending work.

Set the First Plan

Confirm inspections, spares, trade contacts and priorities with LGES. Inspection checklist, spares and trade contacts, agreed initial priorities.

SPECIALTY

Doors, Shutters & Low-Voltage Interfaces

Doors & Shutters

Record visible damage, operator condition, seals and reported interlock faults. Specialist checks: safety devices and post-repair function.

Low-Voltage Interfaces

Identify the affected device, fault indication and known connection interface. Preserve approved settings and retain the diagnosis record.

Repair & Warranty

Check whether correction belongs to construction warranty or maintenance. Retain trade reports, test evidence and remaining follow-up.

RESPONSE

A Response Matched to the Reported Condition

Emergency

Immediate safety or critical operating concern. Contain the risk within authorization. Notify LGES and escalate the required response.

Urgent

Issue affecting use or likely to worsen. Inspect and assess the effect. Agree repair or specialist attendance with the client.

Routine

Defect suitable for planned maintenance. Prepare authorized work and materials. Schedule the repair and verify completion.
